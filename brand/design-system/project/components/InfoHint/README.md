An info hint: a small i beside a label that opens a short explanation, by hover, focus or press.

## Use

- The i is 20px, `ink-secondary`, beside a `type-label` ("Total", "Needs review").
- The bubble is `panel` with `radius-lg`, `shadow-pop` and a `line` edge, 260px wide, `type-caption` in `ink`.
- One or two sentences that say how a figure is worked out or what a state means: "Refunds are subtracted. Each payment counts once, even when several documents show it."
- It is a toggletip: hover or focus opens it, a press toggles it, Escape closes it. The button is named "About the total" and carries `aria-expanded`.

## You provide

The label and the explanation. Library has no InfoHint today; the first ones go on a chart's total and on Needs review.
