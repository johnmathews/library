# App icon and logo

**Question:** which mark goes on Library's app icon, favicon and sidebar logo. The square is Family
Finances' violet app-icon square (`brand-fill`, mark in `on-brand`), so the two apps read as one
family; only the mark is open. The logo is that square beside "Library" in Inter, sentence case.

**Status:** decided (2026-11-21)

**Link:** https://claude.ai/artifact/DXEPvR8phNfS4oFG5FkEXY

## Options

- **A. A solid sheet under a lens.** A white page with three lines cut out, a lens over its lower
  corner. Recommended: filled like Finances' bars, so it holds up best at 16px and the two icons
  look drawn by the same hand.
- **B. A page drawn in lines.** Round-capped strokes: a page with a folded corner and two lines, the
  lens over the corner. The most "document" at 64px and up, the thinnest at 16px.
- **C. Writing inside a lens.** Only the magnifying glass, with lines inside it. Clearest at 16px,
  but close to the sidebar's own Search icon.

All three: the mark on the square is 6.1:1 in both themes. "Library" on the sidebar is 14.7:1 in
light and 14.5:1 in dark.

## Decision

**B · A page drawn in lines**, 2026-11-21. The owner picked it from the three options, against
the recommended A.

## Rounds

- **1, 2026-11-21:** three marks, each in a browser tab at 16px beside Family Finances' tab, at
  16–120px, in the sidebar's logo (expanded and collapsed) and on a phone home screen beside
  Finances, in both themes. Option A's lens was emptied before publishing: one line inside it read
  as a "zoom out" minus.
