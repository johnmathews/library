Empty months, axis money and the last raw colours

Three small questions. The ChartStyle card promises a stub for an empty month and euro ticks that the chart doesn't draw, so either the chart learns them or the card drops them. And 23 colours are still written as rgb() (markdown code, tables and the thin scrollbar), which the tightened colour guard will refuse. Everything else on the chart and the notes stays as it is. All figures here are made up.
